Htr2aA242S-APEX2-Cre · electron-microscopic localization
● Prototype · preview only

APEX2 fused to the receptor oxidises DAB into an electron-dense polymer that stays where the enzyme is. The receptor therefore appears as dark reaction product on identifiable membranes, placing it at a specific compartment of the synapse rather than somewhere in the neuropil.

APEX2-DAB reaction product at synaptic membranes. Scale bars 50 nm.
